`1.01` to `2.10`, so all 36 of our ordinals resolve to an exact board rank. A name-based
join finds none of them — match the prefix. This is the primary pick source

## Chart cliff — measured, not remembered

Walking `pick-values.csv` band midpoints through `V()` at `D` = 456, largest steps down:

| # | Step | Δ value |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | 1.04 → **1.05** (band widens Top 25-35 → Top 50-70) | **1684** |
| 2 | 1.01 → 1.02 | 1360 |
| 3 | 1.03 → 1.04 | 1104 |
| 4 | 1.07 → 1.08 | 614 |
| 8 | 1.30 → 2.01 — *the round-1/round-2 boundary* | **201** |

The cliff is at the **top** of the chart, and the round boundary is only the 8th largest
step, **8.4× smaller** than the real one. `V()`'s convexity, not the class: a 30-rank step
at rank 30 beats a 60-rank step at rank 215. `eval-pick` → *A chart prices ONE class*.
