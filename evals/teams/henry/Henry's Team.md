## Giannis — the headline call, and what it is *not*

He is the largest `Δw ours` on this roster (+1.890) and the largest `Δw thr` (+2.08), on a
projected **52** games against **36** actual. Three things have to be said in order:

1. **`sim.project_gp` has already regressed him** — 36 → 52 is the durability adjustment, applied
   identically on both sides of any deal (`Eval Definitions §Durability`). **The boards have also
   already charged for the injuries**; a second, format-derived fragility discount is forbidden,
   and so is refusing him at every shape (`trades` §*Check before answering*).
2. **The gap is not a signal at our band.** BASE 6552 against `TVAL` 7371 flips at `κ*` **1313**,
   which sits inside our 1205–3339 — so our own numbers do not establish that he is underpriced
   *or* overpriced for us (`Eval Definitions §κ`). The same is true of Edwards (1766) and Mobley
   (1884). Where the file's prior does hold up is the **ordering**: to us he outranks Edwards on
   wins while the boards rank him below.
3. **Two blind spots to flag, not patch** (`Eval Definitions §Durability`). Whole-season risk is
   **censored** — `project_gp` is expected GP *given he plays*, fitted on a pool that a missed
   year leaves out entirely, so it cannot see the branch that matters most on a player with two
   right-soleus strains in one season. And the fit is weakest on fragment seasons; his 36 is
   above the 10–25 band, but **Pippen (10 → 40)** on this same roster is squarely inside it.

`LATE` reads **✓** — one of five rotation seasons under 60. That is a tiebreak and nothing more:
it may not be sized, folded into `Δw`/`TVAL`, or used to reprice him.

**So the honest position is not "avoid Giannis" — it is that no multi-body shape clears.** At 38
men the incoming rate needed is **41.0 at 3-for-1 and 48.8 at 4-for-1** for a 68-GP forward
(`evals/lineup-math/findings.md` §*Break-evens*); he supplies 48.9 on a projected **52** games, and
that ladder's own GP table (a 48.9-rate forward is +1.03 wins at 45 GP against +1.89 at 65)
puts his 52-GP break-even **above** the printed 68-GP row. Read against the same file's
consolidation ladder, a 4- or 5-body price for him is where the sign goes negative.
**Negotiate the shape, not the player: 1-for-1, or 2-for-1 with a pick, and 3-for-1 only out of
genuine dregs.**

Sensitivity, since the window call is his alone: truncating '28-29 (age 34) takes `TVAL` 7371 →
**5,233**, still above the BASE the market charges. That is stated as a bound, not adopted —
truncation here would double-count the trajectory BASE already prices (`Eval Definitions §TVAL`,
§Horizon).
