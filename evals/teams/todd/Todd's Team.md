## The perception gap — sell it, don't buy it

This is the tradeable thing on this roster, and it runs two ways off one question: **does
Todd read 0-19 as a rebuild, or as an injury year he is about to bounce out of?**

- **The market's error is buying the picks.** Han finished last, so their futures get priced
  as lottery tickets. The projections say otherwise: their Sept-'26 **1.01 is real and locked**
  at 6,757, but their '27/'28/'29 firsts price at roughly **half** that. **Do not pay
  1.01-adjacent prices for a Han future first** — and equally, do not dismiss them: they are
  one of only three genuinely valuable pick sources in this league, so price them, at ~3,000,
  rather than waving at them.
- **If he reads rebuild**, Gobert (34), Dunn (32), McConnell (34), Lopez (38) and Clarkson
  (34) all get cheap, and Dëmin/Bryant/Walter/Konan Niederhäuser become unbuyable at any
  price. That is the branch our target list below is built on.
- **If he reads bounce-back**, the sellable thing is *our* youth into his window, and Brunson
  moves from a mid `GET` to unbuyable. Sound it out before anchoring — the two branches price
  Brunson 3,589 apart.
