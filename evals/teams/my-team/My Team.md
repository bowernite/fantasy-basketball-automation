### Reading the two tables

**Both baselines run through the 2026 class's board rows** — step 2 of `eval-pick`
§*Future picks*, "always available, wrong by the amount you are sizing." Corrections, in order
of how well each is sourced:

- **Class strength, '27 against '26 — direction sourced, magnitude not.** The 2026 class is
  strong (3–5 likely All-Stars); ESPN calls 2027 "indeterminate", with no prospect currently
  projecting as a guaranteed lottery pick (`evals/Team Projections.md` caveats 2 and 3). I apply
  a **judgment haircut of ~15–25%** to every Sept-'27 row, flagged as the one discretionary cut
  in this file. A bear case, not an estimate.
- **Year decay, '28 against '27 — there is no such factor, and this is the trap.** `dynatyze` is
  the only external price on a future pick, and its slot curve is a parametric template,
  numerically identical across years; only the 1.01 anchor and the trade blend are
  class-specific. So **do not discount Sept '28 against Sept '27 by a year factor** — the only
  honest difference is class strength, and nothing prices the 2028 class at all. The available
  snapshot (`evals/board-snapshots/Boards 2026-07-29.md`) publishes two rows off the coarse
  inline `PICK` buckets: `2027 Early 1st` rank **41** → `V` **3270**, `2027 Mid 1st` rank **72**
  → `V` **2021**. **No late-1st row, no round 2+, no 2028 row**, and the per-cell trade weight
  `w` is unrecorded, so expect template-only (`w = 0`) on any 2028 round-2/-3 cell.
- **`Mid 1st` is a ceiling for our 1sts, never a price.** Ours land **1.11** in both years —
  late, not mid. Dynatyze's 2021 at the mid sits above the 1425 the '26 class implies for 1.11,
  which says the modelled baseline is not obviously too high and says nothing more.
- **Effective haircut `h`** is **~15–25% on Sept '27, and the same ~15–25% carried to Sept '28,
  not compounded** — the '27 cut is for a class ESPN has looked at, and a second year of
  discount would be invented. Flat across our own holdings, because every pick we hold is late,
  where classes differ least. **The residual uncertainty on the '28 rows is wider and is not
  expressed as a lower number.**

- **A prior is not its mode.** `V()` is convex, so The Don's 2nd (2.05–2.11) and King
  Christopher's 2nd (2.02–2.09) are worth **more** than their modal slot priced as a point —
  asymmetric in our favour on both. Named and unsized (`eval-pick` §Traps).

  roster contending in every projected season, that is the whole argument for spending them:
  our 1sts are 1.11 and 1.11, cheap currency, and never to be priced by a counterparty as a
