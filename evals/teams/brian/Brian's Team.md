## The read

**Fringe and drifting.** Projected **8th ('26-27) / 9th ('27-28) / 7th ('28-29)** — one
superstar, a pile of 26–33 year old mid-tier producers behind him, and not one young player
who projects as a future 40+ asset. Podziemski (23.4, 27.1) and Braun (25.3, 22.7) are the
closest things to a pipeline and neither has that tail. Flat production in a rising league is
what the drift is.

**The unresolved tension, stated rather than resolved.** `evals/Team Projections.md` records
two independently computed availability-adjusted baselines that both rank this the **strongest
roster in the league**, on Jokić alone, against the **8th–9th** projection filed there. Both
cannot be right, and the file does not pick. The consequence for us is one-directional and
large: **if Jokić plays 75 games this is a top-4 team and their picks are worthless.** Their
Sept-'28 1st is the only holding whose price depends on which reading is true, and it is
priced below on the filed projection, not on the baseline.

**Jokić.** 65.2 FPts/G on 65 GP, C-only — the only player in the league over 60. `Δw ours`
**+3.266**, the largest single figure on any roster in this exercise, against **BASE 8,853**
and **`TVAL` 12,739**. κ* is **1,026**, below our band, so `TVAL` > BASE holds across the
whole band rather than at the point estimate. `GET` is near-zero: `Team Projections.md` calls
his health *"their entire floor"* and a 45-game Jokić season makes this a lottery team
outright. ⚠️ **Read any consolidation for him against the 38-roster · 65-GP-centre row of
`evals/lineup-math/findings.md` §*Break-evens*, never the 68-GP-forward row** — that file states
the forward row inverts the sign of his 5-for-1.

⚠️ **Deandre Ayton's 26.7 is stale.** He was demoted to backup centre in Washington; the
backbone carried his last-season rate forward, so **his `WINS` 0.88, `Δw` and `TVAL` 830 are
all upper bounds on a role that no longer exists.** The correction belongs in the `WINS` layer
per `eval-player`'s Role carve-out — it is **not** an injury adjustment and **not** a BASE
adjustment, so BASE 940 stands as published. Row flagged rather than rewritten: we do not have
a sourced post-demotion rate. Do not let anyone price him at 26.7, and do not pay his BASE.

**Their `κ` is 722** against our 1477 — this roster converts BASE into wins at roughly half

about their roster shape, not a price; nothing here is valued with it.
