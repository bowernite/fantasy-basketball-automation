## Situation

**Contending, hard, right now — and falling off a cliff inside our window.** 14-5, **28,502
PF**, the best actual scoring team in the league two years running. Projected **1st in '26-27,
5th in '27-28, 9th in '28-29**. `Mₜ` is 1.0 for them today and roughly 0 by '28-29.

Three genuine 41+ FPts anchors — Towns, Murray, Mitchell — which **nobody else in the league
has.** Embiid and Lillard are two more 42+ rates behind them, on 38 and 0 games respectively.

**The defining fact is the pipeline: there isn't one.** No player under 26 with a top-30 tail.
Barrett at 26.1 is the youngest meaningful producer and he is a 30.6, not a future anchor. Seven
players are 32+ today; six more sit at 31 and cross 32 during '27-28. That is a **synchronised**
cliff, not a gradual one, and it lands squarely on the back half of our horizon.

### The read — and it runs the wrong way from the obvious one

Contending now with no pipeline means **they need their stars more, not less**, until the cliff
actually arrives. There is no young replacement waiting and no incoming pick to become one: both
their Sept-'26 1st and 2nd are already gone, and so is their Sept-'27 1st.

So Towns (`κ*` 744), Murray (853) and Mitchell (1,046) are `GET`-**high** despite `TVAL`
comfortably clearing BASE on all three. Per `trades` §*What other owners price on*, `GET` high +
`TVAL` high is **pay up or walk** — not a mispricing, and not a cheap-target list. Do not open on
them expecting a discount.

**What they actually want is youth and future picks.** That is the axis any deal with them runs
on, and it is the axis we are shortest on after our own dealings — our future 1sts are 1.09–1.11
every year of the window (`evals/Team Projections.md`) and our young core is the same thing as
our contending core. Expect to pay in bodies and current production, or not at all.
