## The read

**Contending, and rising.** 10-10 on record but **2nd in points-for** over a 19-game
schedule — the largest record/PF divergence in the league, and `evals/Team Projections.md`
§*Confidence* names this roster as its own worked example of why W-L is not the load-bearing
signal. Projected **4th / 1st / 3rd** across our window, so their `Mₜ` is high in every
season we care about and they buy like a contender in all three.

**Twenty of the 28 rows sit at 20+ FPts/G** — counted off the table above, and it is
knife-edge on one row: Huerter is 20.01 and LaRavia 19.54. In a 9-slot daily format that
breadth is a real structural edge, and `evals/lineup-math/findings.md` §*The slot-fill curve*
says exactly where it cashes: on light nights nearly everyone available starts, so a
26-FPts body and an 8-FPts body differ by the full 18 — *presence, not rate* — and **88% of
breadth's payoff sits on ~32 nights** (67% on just 14). That measurement is on our own
38-man basis, but the mechanism is the format's.

**The edge is largely a '26-27 edge.** Same file, §*Sept '26 expansion*: **breadth stops
being a differentiator once every team fills to 38 out of the same auction pool**, and
filling those slots is worth +2.14 wins to everyone for free. Depth held today buys nothing
durable. Their advantage is real this season and mostly gone by '27-28 — which is worth
knowing before paying a premium for it.

**The catch is the top end.** Exactly one row on this roster clears 40 FPts/G at all —
**Tyrese Maxey** at 46.4, top of the `Δw ours` ordering at +1.869. Banchero at 39.0 is next,
and after him the roster is a wall of 25–35s.
