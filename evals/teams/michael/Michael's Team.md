## Sourced vs modelled

Per-board ranks, BASE's inputs, `FPts/G`, `GP` actual, `AGE`, `ELIG`, body count and the Sept-'26
slots and their originators are **sourced**. `GP proj`, `WINS`, both `Δw` columns, `REPL`, `K`,

`GET` and `ACQ` are judgment and are neither. No discount was applied anywhere in this file —
the seven truncated windows are `Eval Definitions §TVAL`'s per-player truncation, not a haircut,
and no injury or fragility adjustment sits on any BASE.
