## Sourced vs modelled

Per-board ranks and BASE's inputs, `FPts/G`, actual `GP`, `AGE`, `ELIG`, body count, pick
ownership and the Sept-'26 ordinals are **sourced**. `GP proj`, `WINS`, both `Δw` columns, `REPL`,
`K`, `κ` (point and band), `TVAL`, `LATE` and every pick VALUE are **modelled**. `GET` and `ACQ`
are judgment and are not values — never summed, never in VERDICT. BASE and `TVAL` sit adjacent
because the gap is the signal; they do not bracket a range
(`Eval Definitions §Sourced vs modelled`).

Discounts chosen here: the **1-year window on Paul George** (age 36, role loss) and the **2-year
window on Anthony Davis and Marcus Smart** (both cross 34 inside the horizon), each printed both
ways above. Every other row is flat across all three seasons — a choice, not a default.
